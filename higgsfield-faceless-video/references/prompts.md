# Scene and narration guidance

Choose a single visual identity, then write one distinct, readable scene for each narration block. For an explainer, let each visual illustrate its specific idea; use diagrams when useful and avoid unnecessary cuts. For a story, maintain characters, location continuity and a clear sequence. Describe motion from the opening frame, camera and composition; keep spoken dialogue and text out of background clips unless the user explicitly requests them. Avoid inventing facts or copying a reference image's identity when it was supplied only for style.

Use the same selected narrator and voice pair throughout. Write natural spoken language in the requested language, keeping each take within its planned window. Do not pad narration with repeated phrases. The final block may be shorter than ten seconds; plan its text for the shorter window rather than trimming speech.
